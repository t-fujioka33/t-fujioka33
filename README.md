## About

I'm an AI research engineer and entrepreneur based in Tokyo. I founded **NextSpark Inc.** in May 2026 and develop **Surutasu**, an iOS app for habit formation and task management.

My research background lies in machine learning for speech, natural language, and visual data — with a particular focus on emotion recognition and human communication. During my time at **Hitachi, Ltd. (2016–2022)**, I worked on speech synthesis, speech emotion recognition, environmental sound recognition, and audio-visual multimodal modeling.

Drawing on my background in engineering and psychology, I also bring a perspective on how people perceive and engage with technology.

## Current Work

- **NextSpark Inc.** — Founder & CEO
- **Surutasu** (iOS) — AI-powered task management with personalized decomposition based on life rhythm, condition, and mood
- **AI Business Development & R&D Support** — Helping organizations bridge research insights and product implementation
